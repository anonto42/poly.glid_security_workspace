# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want PolyGlid to be a welcoming, respectful, and useful project for
contributors from different backgrounds, skill levels, and communities.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, especially across language and cultural differences.
- Give feedback about code and ideas, not personal character.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project.
- Respect privacy and do not share private project files, credentials, or
  personal data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing secrets, tokens, private project data, or other sensitive material.
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive comments.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other
contributions that violate this code of conduct. Maintainers may also limit or
block participation when needed to protect the project community.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. For security concerns,
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
